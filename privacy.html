Linkchat Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

What we store

When you create an account we store your chosen username and a password (stored by Google Firebase, not readable by us). We store the messages you send, the chats and groups you belong to, and the time of each message. Linkchat does not ask for your real name, email address or phone number.

Messages are not end-to-end encrypted

Messages are stored on Google Firebase servers and can be accessed by the app owner and by Google as the hosting provider. Do not send passwords or other highly sensitive information in Linkchat.

Links and videos

When a message contains a link, your device may contact that website to show a preview or play a video. That website can see your device's IP address.

Advertising

Linkchat shows ads from Start.io. Start.io may collect your IP address, device properties, carrier and network information, and, if you agree in the consent box, additional data such as installed apps and approximate location, to show personalised ads. See the Start.io privacy policy at start.io for details and opt-out options.

Children

Linkchat is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Deleting your data

You can delete your own messages inside the app. To request deletion of your account and data, open an issue at github.com/1o4f3t-pixel/Linkchat/issues with your username.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above will change when we do.
