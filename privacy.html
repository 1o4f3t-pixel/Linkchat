Linkchat Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

What we store

When you create an account we store your chosen username and a password (stored by Google Firebase, not readable by us). We store your chats and groups, group names, the usernames of members, message timestamps, and your public encryption key. Linkchat does not ask for your real name, email address or phone number.

Message encryption

Since version 1.3, the text of messages is end-to-end encrypted. Messages are scrambled on the sender's phone and can only be unscrambled on the phones of the people in that chat. The keys needed to read them stay on your device and are never sent to us, so neither the app owner nor Google (our hosting provider) can read message text.

Not encrypted: usernames, who is in which chat, group names, and the time messages are sent. Messages sent before version 1.3 are not encrypted.

Because the keys exist only on your device, if you uninstall Linkchat, clear its data or change phones, you will not be able to read your older messages. We cannot recover them. Linkchat does not yet let you verify that a contact's key is genuine.

Links and videos

When a message contains a link, your device may contact that website to show a preview or play a video. That website can see your device's IP address.

Advertising

Linkchat shows ads from Start.io. Start.io may collect your IP address, device properties, carrier and network information, and, if you agree in the consent box, additional data such as installed apps and approximate location, to show personalised ads. Start.io does not receive your message text. See the Start.io privacy policy at start.io for details and opt-out options.

Children

Linkchat is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Deleting your data

You can delete your own messages inside the app. To request deletion of your account and data, open an issue at github.com/1o4f3t-pixel/Linkchat/issues with your username.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above will change when we do.
